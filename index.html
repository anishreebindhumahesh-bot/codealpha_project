What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers pretend to be trustworthy people or organizations to trick you into revealing sensitive information.

Common Warning Signs

  • 🚨 Urgent or threatening messages
  • 🔗 Suspicious links
  • 📧 Unknown or unusual sender addresses
  • 🔑 Requests for passwords or OTPs
  • 📎 Unexpected attachments
  • 💰 Unexpected requests for money

Stay Safe

Never share your password or OTP. Verify suspicious requests through an official website or trusted contact.
