🛡️ Phishing Awareness Training

Learn to recognize phishing and stay safe online.

🎣 What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals pretend to be trustworthy people or organizations to trick people into sharing sensitive information.

🚨 Common Warning Signs

  • Suspicious sender email address
  • Urgent or threatening messages
  • Unexpected links or attachments
  • Requests for passwords or OTPs
  • Requests for money or financial information
  • Spelling or grammar mistakes

🌐 How to Identify a Fake Website

  • Check the website address carefully.
  • Look for unusual or misspelled domain names.
  • Do not trust unexpected login pages.
  • Don't enter passwords through suspicious links.
  • Open important websites directly instead of using unknown links.

🧠 Social Engineering

Attackers often manipulate people rather than directly attacking technology.

  • Fear
  • Urgency
  • Fake authority
  • Rewards and prizes
  • Curiosity

⚠️ Remember

Never share your password, PIN or OTP with someone who unexpectedly asks for it.
